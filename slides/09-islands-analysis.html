Collecting the data

Our sampling plan

You will adapt the stratified cluster design:

  1. Different Islands might have drastically different life expectancies so let’s make sure we sample individuals from each island (i.e. stratify by island)
  2. Individual villages within each Island may also have some variation in life expectancy. However, it will be too difficult to sample EVERY village so we’ll treat these as clusters.
  3. Each person in this class will be randomly assigned one village.
  4. Within each village, you will record data on 10 randomly collected islander.

Overall Sampling Plan

Multistage sample:

  • Stage 1: Stratify by island
  • Stage 2: Cluster by village
  • Stage 3: Simple random sample from those who died between 375 and 399
